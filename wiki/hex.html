Hexerella "Hex" Hellfyre is a Brooklyn-born operative and one of the central figures in the recovered Teknoa records. She appears throughout as Oms' confidant and Tekna's closest friend, and is credited across the arcs with a precision at thrown weapons that the records treat as its own kind of anomaly.

She is easy to underestimate. The records suggest she prefers it that way.

Appearance

Hex stands 5 ft 3 in (160 cm). Her hair is a violet-and-magenta split dye she has kept through every recorded arc, worn loose or gathered depending on the day. Her eyes are heterochromatic and invert the colours of her hair — one magenta, one violet — an inversion the records note without ever explaining.

Her makeup is heavy, violet and black. A small motif sits beneath one eye; whether it is ink, scar or paint is not recorded.

She favours a black and violet pinstripe corset, short-shorts and fishnets, platforms and striped knee-high socks, and is rarely without a choker. Combat padding at the knuckles, elbows and knees appears in almost every account — including those where no engagement was expected.

Accounts agree, with unusual consistency, on how she smells: bubblegum, cinnamon, and gasoline.

Personality

Hex presents as scattered, loud and extremely forward. The records are clear that this is at least partly a choice. Observers describe her as ditzy yet very self-aware, and the same accounts that call her unserious note that she is almost never wrong about a room.

Her speech is fast and dense with invented metaphor, delivered in a broad Brooklyn accent she has made no attempt to lose. She is funny on purpose and disarming by habit, and both are load-bearing.

Her attachment to Oms is the defining feature of her character. The records treat it less as infatuation than as orientation — a fixed point she navigates by, and the thing most likely to make her act against her own interests.

Skills and equipment

Hex's declared specialty is accuracy. She carries custom throwing knives balanced to her own specification, and the arcs credit her with hits at distances that should not be available to a thrown blade.

Her second discipline is explosives, where the records note her for placement rather than yield. She wears no armour beyond combat padding, relying instead on not being where she was.

Role

Hex is the deuteragonist of the recovered records — the figure whose presence most often shapes Oms' decisions without directing them. Her position between Oms and Tekna, confidant to one and closest friend to the other, holds for the whole of the recovered timeline.
