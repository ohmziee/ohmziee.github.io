Renard "Ren" Ti-Kalfu is a Gonaïves-born engineer, founder of a small medical-technology startup, and the closest thing Oms has to an older brother. In the recovered records he is the one who fixes things — machines, plans, and on a bad night, people.

He treats Hex like a sister and Gilly like a sparring partner, and both arrangements appear to suit everyone.

Appearance

Ren stands 6 ft 2 in (188 cm), strong and quick on his feet. His skin is a deep umber. He wears his hair in side cornrows over a fade, and almost every account mentions the same expression: a sly half-smile that suggests he already knows how the conversation ends.

He dresses better than the situation calls for, every time. A fine black suit with blue trim, black dress shoes with blue soles, and subtle vèvè patterns worked into the lining where only someone standing very close would see them.

He carries an old pocket watch on a chain, and he is rarely recorded without it.

Accounts agree on how he smells: coconut, cedarwood and black pepper.

Personality

Ren is fun and fiercely loyal, and the records describe him as a resourceful genius of the improvised kind — the person who builds what the crew needs out of whatever is in the room. He talks smoothly, with a slow, almost hypnotic cadence, in an accent shaped by Haitian Creole that slips further into Kreyòl when he is tired, amused or annoyed.

With Oms he is protective in a way he never announces. With Gilly he argues about everything, constantly, and the records suggest neither of them would know what to do if the other ever agreed.

Work

Ren runs a med-tech startup out of Brooklyn. The records are vague about what it makes, which may be deliberate. They are clear that it pays for a great deal of what the crew does, and that Ren has never once asked to be paid back.

He lives with the others at the lighthouse in Coney Island, where most of the workshop is his.

The watch

Ren's pocket watch is the one object he will not lend, explain or let anyone else hold. It is old, heavy, and engraved on the back with a pattern no one has managed to photograph clearly.

The records note that Ren has never been seen winding it. They also note that it has never been seen to stop. One account mentions that it ran several minutes behind for an entire evening, and that Ren spent that evening unusually quiet, checking it often. Nothing in the account explains why, and the next day it was right again.

Skills and equipment

His declared specialty is intellect. Ren solves problems faster than most people can describe them, and prefers solutions nobody else would have thought to try.

He carries dual hidden daggers. The records note that most people who meet him never learn this, and that the few who do usually learn it all at once.
