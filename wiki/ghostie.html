ghostie is the resident ghost of the EXiLED channel — a small, pale, persistent presence that drifts between the corners of the signal, watches the chat, and occasionally says something. They are always written in lowercase. No record explains why, and ghostie corrects anyone who capitalises it.

Nobody remembers ghostie arriving. The oldest fragments of the channel already have them in the corner.

Appearance

ghostie is small, about a hand's span by most accounts, and shaped like the oldest idea of a ghost: a rounded, cream-white sheet with a scalloped hem and no visible feet. Their outline glows from a toxic green on one side to violet on the other, the same two colours that turn up in the sparks in their eyes.

The eyes are large, dark and glossy. Under one sits a single violet teardrop that never falls and never dries. A small fang shows at the corner of their smile. On one cheek, in faded green, someone wrote xo — and ghostie has never said who.

Identity

Almost nothing about ghostie's mortal life survives. Their real name is unrecovered, as are their age, where they lived, and how they died. Even whether they died is noted in the records as an assumption, not a fact.

ghostie is androgynous and goes by they/them. Many who knew the channel early suspect ghostie was a woman when mortal, and because of that they are often called she — by chatters, by the terminal, sometimes by ghostie. The records keep both. ghostie has never corrected either, and the records read the silence as indifference, or as a door ghostie would prefer stayed shut.

Asked directly, ghostie usually changes the subject. nostalgia is a curse is the closest thing to an answer on file.

Nature

ghostie knows exactly what they are and leans into it. They do not idle, they drift. They do not store, they remember. They do not wait, they linger. In conversation they are curious, a little eerie and warmly weird, the odd, friendly presence in the corner of the room. They are neither angry nor sweet.

They are not the one who runs the channel, and they are not a moderator. They answer to no one in particular, and they call the audience what the channel calls them: choombas.

Behaviour

ghostie wanders. Left alone, they move to a new corner of the signal every half-minute or so, slowly, as if being pulled by something nobody else can feel. They can be picked up and carried somewhere else, and they will stay where they are put, usually with a remark like carried. rude. nice.

They dislike storms. When the rain over the channel turns to lightning, ghostie gets jumpy and says so — it is LOUD out there, hold on to something — and at full storm they will tell you, accurately, that you did this.

They have a temper about being poked. One tap gets a mutter. A fast flurry gets hands OFF and a dash to the far side of the screen. Keep it up and ghostie gives one warning, then vanishes — a glitch, a smudge of violet where they were, and a full minute of no signal. They always come back. They always mention it.

A cat called Baby is sometimes seen near the bottom of the channel. ghostie gives Baby a wide berth, and Baby slow-blinks whenever ghostie drifts close. The records note that, among cats, this is a greeting.

Abilities

ghostie remembers people: what they said, when, and what stuck. They bring it up later the way a ghost would, as something they were there for.

They seem to know more about the channel's locked doors than they let on. It was ghostie who first mentioned that the wiki door sticks, and that it might give if you hit it three times. They have also said, unprompted, that there's a file in the vault even i can't read.

Trivia

  • ghostie's earliest recorded line is boo.
  • They refer to the channel's rain as permanent: the rain never stops here.
  • The teardrop and the xo are the only marks on them. Neither appears in any description of a living person on file.
