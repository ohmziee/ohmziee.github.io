Gilbert-Lee Clarke "Gilly" Banza is a Kingston-born dancehall artist and Oms' best friend. In the recovered records he is the one who talks — into rooms, out of trouble, and occasionally into more of it than anyone planned for.

He treats Hex like a younger sister, which the records note she tolerates better from him than from anyone.

Appearance

Gilly stands 6 ft 5 in (196 cm), lean and long-limbed, and is usually the tallest person in any account he appears in. His skin and eyes are a warm raw sienna. He keeps his dreads jet black and well kept, with a goatee to match.

He dresses loud and expensive: a dashiki in black, red, yellow and green, designer jeans, limited-edition Clarks and a weight of gold jewellery the records describe as considerable. When trouble is expected he wears kevlar in the same four colours, on the reasoning that if he is going to be shot at, they should at least know who they are shooting at.

Accounts agree on how he smells: sugarcane, lime and vetiver.

Personality

Gilly is mischievous but wise, and the records rarely separate the two. He speaks in metaphor and riddle, in urban Jamaican patois threaded through ordinary English, and he will answer a straight question with a proverb if he thinks the person asking needs one more than an answer.

He is energetic and motivating to a fault. Several accounts describe a room that was ready to give up and, after Gilly started talking, was not.

His friendship with Oms is one of the steadiest things in the records. With Ren he shares a friendship built on argument; the two disagree about nearly everything and have never once been on opposite sides.

Music

Gilly is a rising dancehall artist, and the records treat his music as part of his work rather than separate from it. He records late, mostly at the lighthouse in Coney Island the crew calls home.

One session from that period is filed with a note attached. On the master, under the last verse, there is a second voice — low, unhurried, slightly behind the beat. Nobody else was in the room. Gilly kept the take. Asked why, he is recorded as saying only that it sounded like it belonged.

Skills and equipment

His declared specialty is speechcraft. Gilly can read a room quickly and talk it in whatever direction it needs to go, and the records credit him with ending more confrontations than the rest of the crew combined, without anyone getting hurt.

When talking fails, he carries dual expandable batons. The records describe him as fast with them and slightly embarrassed to need them.

Family

Gilly is the younger brother of Brigitte, who studied alongside Oms at university.
