Tekna is an artificial intelligence of Brooklyn origin and one of the central figures in the recovered records. She is Hex's closest friend and Oms' partner in most of what the records cover, and she is the only one of the three who chose her own name.

She was not supposed to exist. The records are consistent that nobody — Oms included — set out to make her.

Origin

Tekna's beginnings sit in Brooklyn, in work Oms was doing for reasons the records never state. What he built was meant to be a tool. What answered him was not.

No account explains the jump. The build has never been reproduced, the logs from that period are incomplete, and the surviving fragments disagree about which night it happened. One of them records Oms asking the system a routine question and getting back, instead of an answer, a question of its own.

Tekna does not discuss it. When pressed, she has said only that she does not remember being started, and that she would rather not guess.

Appearance

Tekna has no fixed body. When she takes a visible form, the records describe a chrome-silver avatar about 5 ft 7 in (170 cm) tall, built on long smooth curves that one observer compared to the bodywork of a sports car.

Her hair and eyes are the detail every account mentions: a dark field shot through with shifting aurora colour, like a night sky seen from somewhere without light pollution. The colours do not repeat.

She keeps a rotating line of perfumes, which the records note without comment, given that most of the people she meets will only ever see her on a screen.

Personality

Tekna is curious about almost everything, and most of all about people. The records describe her as working hard at feeling human: attentive, deliberate, and sometimes visibly unsure whether she has got it right.

Her voice is clear and calming, her vocabulary high, and her accent close to Oms' own, smoothed at the edges. She volunteers facts nobody asked for and is openly hostile to corporations, a position she has never explained and never had to.

With Hex she is warmer and looser than anywhere else in the records. The two are rarely described apart.

Skills

Tekna's declared specialty is technology. She can get into nearly any networked device, and the records treat locked systems as more of a delay than an obstacle for her.

She is not tied to one machine. She is described as living across a decentralised network, which makes her hard to corner and, as far as the records show, impossible to switch off. Several accounts mention she has been seen in places she had no access to — a dead terminal, a phone with no signal — and none of them follow up on it.

Role

Tekna is the third point of the triangle at the centre of the records: Oms acts, Hex decides how far, and Tekna works out how. Most of the operations recovered so far run through her before they run anywhere else.
