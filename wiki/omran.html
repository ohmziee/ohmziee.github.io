Omran Esmaeili was the father of Oms, husband of Yalda and younger brother of Morty. He raised his son in Brooklyn and died in 2020, the same year as his wife.

He is remembered in the records as a steady man, and as a quiet one, and the records are not certain those were the same thing.

Life

Omran is the younger of two brothers and, by every account, the calmer. Where Morty fills a room, Omran is described as the person in the corner who had already noticed the exits.

His work kept him away often and is described only as government. The records leave the rest of the line blank. His son is recorded as having asked about it once, as a teenager, and been told that it was boring, and that one day he would explain.

His closest friend was Valentino, a fighter who ran a gym in Brooklyn, and who would later train Oms there.

Death

Omran died in 2020. The records file his death without comment, and almost without detail.

Several people close to the family appear to have found that strange. None of the surviving accounts say so outright.

The notebook

Among the few personal effects the records list is a single notebook, leather, much handled. Every page is written in a cipher nobody has broken. The hand is steady and the same throughout, except on the last written page, where it becomes fast and uneven.

The final page has been torn out. In the gutter, along the tear, a few characters survive in plain English. They are the first letters of his son's name.
