Yalda Esmaeili was the mother of Oms and the wife of Omran. She raised her son in Brooklyn and is listed in the recovered records as having died in 2020, the same year as her husband, during the lockdowns.

Of everyone in the records, she is the one Oms speaks about least. The records suggest it is not because there is little to say.

Life

Almost nothing about Yalda's early life survives. The records do not say where she was born, how she met Omran, or what she did before her son was born in Brooklyn in 2000.

What they do preserve is how she raised him. In 2016, after Oms lost his closest childhood friend, it was Yalda who walked him into a combat gym in Brooklyn called Pura Vida and signed him up. The records do not say why she chose a fighting gym for a grieving boy. They do note that it worked.

Death

Yalda is recorded as having died in 2020. The record is brief, and the entry for cause is empty.

Her son has never contested it. He has also never been recorded visiting a grave.

The calls

After 2020, Oms' phone shows a handful of missed calls from his mother's number. They come late, never more than one a night, and months apart.

The number was disconnected in 2020. The carrier has no record of it being reassigned. When Oms calls back, the line does not ring and does not fail. It simply stays open, and quiet, until he hangs up.

He has never told anyone what he hears in it. The records note only that he has never once blocked the number.
